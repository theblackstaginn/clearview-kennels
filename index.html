Good dogs begin with a good start.

At Clearview, puppies are raised with attention, affection, and the kind of everyday care that helps prepare them for life with their future families.

We raise Cavalier King Charles Spaniels and Cavapoos in Marshfield, Missouri, and work with families both nearby and across the country.

Raised at Clearview

More than a puppy. A new member of the family.

The first weeks of a puppy's life matter. They shape how that puppy experiences people, routines, and the world around them.

Clearview's goal is simple: give every puppy a thoughtful beginning and help each family find the companion that feels right for their home.

Our Breeds

Two wonderful ways to find your companion.

Clearview specializes in Cavalier King Charles Spaniels and Cavapoos.

Cavalier King Charles Spaniel

Gentle, affectionate, and devoted.

Cavaliers are known for their sweet nature and close bond with their people. They bring the warmth of a companion dog into a remarkably beautiful little spaniel.

View Cavaliers

Cavapoo

Bright, loving, and full of personality.

Cavapoos combine the affectionate personality of the Cavalier with the intelligence and playful nature of the Poodle.

View Cavapoos
