Our Story

A Missouri kennel built around the dogs.

Clearview is home to Cavaliers and Cavapoos and the people who care for them.

Choosing a puppy is a big decision. Clearview wants families to have the information they need, understand the process, and feel comfortable asking questions along the way.

From meeting available puppies to arranging the trip home, the goal is to keep the experience clear, personal, and straightforward.
