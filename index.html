The People Behind Clearview

A kennel built around the dogs.

The dogs come first. Everything else grows from there.

Choosing a puppy is a big decision, and Clearview wants families to have the information they need to make it thoughtfully.

That means understanding the puppy, knowing what comes next, and having room to ask real questions along the way.

From the first introduction to arranging the trip home, the goal is simple: keep the experience clear, personal, and human.

Home can be a long way from here.

That's okay.

Clearview works with families locally and from farther away. Depending on where you live, bringing your puppy home may mean meeting in Marshfield, arranging ground transportation, or using a flight nanny.

There isn't one right answer for every family. Talk with Clearview and figure out what makes sense for you and your puppy.
