Good to Know

Before you bring them home.

The basics about availability, deposits, transportation, and working with Clearview.

How do I know which puppies are available?

Puppies listed as available on the website are currently looking for families. Availability can change, so contact Clearview if you're interested in a specific puppy.

How much is the deposit?

Approved families may reserve an available puppy with a $300 deposit.

Does submitting an application reserve a puppy?

No. An application begins the conversation with Clearview. A puppy is not considered reserved simply because an application has been submitted.

Where is Clearview Kennels located?

Clearview Kennels is located in Marshfield, Missouri.

What breeds does Clearview raise?

Clearview raises Cavalier King Charles Spaniels and Cavapoos.

Can I get a puppy if I don't live in Missouri?

Clearview works with families both locally and farther away. Depending on your location, personal pickup, ground transportation, or a flight nanny may be options.

Can I ask about an upcoming litter?

Yes. If the puppy you're looking for isn't currently available, contact Clearview and ask about upcoming Cavaliers or Cavapoos.

Can I ask about pedigree or registration information?

Yes. Clearview can provide additional information about a puppy's parents, pedigree, and registration when applicable.
