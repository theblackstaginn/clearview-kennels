Good to Know

Before you apply.

A few of the things families commonly want to know before reaching out.

How do I know which puppies are available?

Puppies shown as available on the Puppies page are currently looking for their families. Availability can change, so Clearview will confirm the puppy's status when speaking with you.

Does applying reserve a puppy?

No. An application starts the conversation. Once an application is approved, Clearview can explain the reservation process for an available puppy.

How much is the deposit?

The current reservation deposit shown on the site is $300 for approved families. Clearview will confirm the terms before any payment is made.

Can I apply if I don't see the right puppy yet?

Yes. You can contact Clearview about upcoming puppies and future litters even when the puppy you're looking for isn't currently listed.

What if I live outside Missouri?

Clearview works with families both locally and farther away. Depending on the situation, personal pickup, ground transportation, or flight-nanny arrangements may be discussed.

Can I ask questions before submitting an application?

Absolutely. Use the contact section below if you have a question first or aren't ready to apply yet.
